Subject: Can we ask this customer again?

Preheader: A useful reference request starts with the customer's time, not your deal deadline.

The sender derives its teaser from the opening article paragraphs. This file documents the expected opening; it is not a separate body override.

The sales team has a deal that might close this week. The buyer wants to hear from someone already using the product. There is one obvious name.

That customer likes the product. She spoke at your event a few months ago. She helped with a story for the website. The account owner thinks she'll say yes to a call.

Then the request comes in: "Can she speak to this prospect tomorrow?"

This is a made-up situation, not a real customer story. But the choice is familiar to anyone in Customer Success (CS).
