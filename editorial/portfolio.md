## Portfolio guardrails

Across each eight-issue window:

- No more than two evidence teardowns.
- At least three issues must build a constructive operating system.
- Every issue has one central argument, one reader decision, and one primary CTA.
- Headlines should not repeat the same contrarian formula.
- First-person experience requires a confirmed entry in `author-experience.md`.
- Every playbook changes a real meeting, decision, or handoff. It cannot be an article summary in worksheet form.
