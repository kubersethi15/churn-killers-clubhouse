# Contributor kit

For partners who accept a pitch and ask for bio, topics and assets. Reusable, so
the same due diligence is not repeated per partner.

**Sourcing rule.** Every claim below maps to `editorial/author-experience.md` or
to a live public Churn Is Dead URL. Nothing is inferred. If a partner asks for
something not here, it needs Kuber's confirmation and a ledger entry first.

**Deliberately absent:** employer names and logos, testimonials, audience-size
figures, customer outcomes, and any numerical result. None are in the ledger, and
several would create problems in a guest bio even if they were.

Both bios use only ledger claims 1, 2, 4 and 5.

**Claim 3, revenue scale, is deliberately excluded.** The ledger records USD 5 to
10 million ARR conservatively as revenue accountability, and whether that
describes a single account band or a total book is still unconfirmed. An
ambiguous number in a bio is worse than no number.

## Canonical link preference

From `editorial/distribution-policy.md`: **the website is the canonical
publication.**

- Preferred: partner publishes an original piece and links to a relevant Churn
  Is Dead URL in the body or bio.
- Acceptable: partner republishes an existing issue with `rel="canonical"`
  pointing at the original churnisdead.com URL.
- Agree originality, attribution and canonical treatment **before** drafting.
  This is already the standing rule on every pitch in the pipeline.
- One tagged link per placement. More than one splits the measurement and reads
  as promotional.

## Disclosure rules

- AI assistance is disclosed where the partner requires it, per the Medium rule
  in the distribution policy.
- No claim about audience size, customer results or commercial outcomes.
- No customer, employer or account identified.
- Anything drawn from Kuber's own experience must already exist in
  `editorial/author-experience.md`. If it does not, it does not ship.
