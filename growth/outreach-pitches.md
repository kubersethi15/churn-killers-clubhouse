## Customer Success Collective: podcast

**To:** contribute@customersuccesscollective.com

**Subject:** Podcast idea: the hidden metric behind Product and CS trust

Hi CSC team,

I publish Churn Is Dead, an evidence-led set of operating systems for Customer Success leaders.

I would like to propose a practical podcast conversation on decision latency: how long a complete customer problem sits without an explicit Product decision. It is a better operating lens than request volume or time to ship because it exposes backlog limbo without asking CS to control the roadmap.

A useful episode could cover:

1. the difference between urgency and decision-quality evidence;
2. the minimum packet CS should bring to Product;
3. four honest decision states: commit, investigate, decline, or request missing evidence;
4. how to preserve trust with customers without inventing roadmap promises.

Listeners would leave with a review format and a decision record they can use immediately. I am happy to adapt the angle to your audience and keep the conversation educational rather than promotional.
