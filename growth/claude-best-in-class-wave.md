# Claude best-in-class growth wave

**Commissioned:** 24 August 2026  
**Owner:** Claude  
**Primary outcome:** qualified Churn Is Dead subscribers who remain active  
**Secondary outcome:** the most trusted, practically useful evidence-led Customer Success publication in its category

This wave adds depth and execution pressure to the existing growth sprint. It
does not replace `claude-growth-sprint.md`, the live manager calendar, or the
experiment register. Claude claims one package at a time, ships a focused PR,
records the next review date, and immediately takes the next unblocked package.

## Immediate order

### BC-01: repair the measurement foundation

Open a corrective PR for merged PR #90. Count unique sessions rather than raw
events, treat site visits and Vault opens as one first-party behavioural source
class, map `pdf-*`, `topic:*`, and `topic-tool:*` resource identifiers to the
right problem, narrow the contamination statement to verified evidence, and
add focused tests.

**Done means:** tests prove host gating, session deduplication, resource mapping,
and the two-independent-source rule. No growth conclusion uses pre-fix rows.

### BC-02: define the category-leading editorial standard

Benchmark at least 12 current public CS, SaaS, Product, RevOps, or management
publications using comparable public evidence. Score them on problem
specificity, source transparency, operating usefulness, originality,
readability, visual explanation, archive discoverability, and reader journey.
Translate the findings into a Churn Is Dead scorecard and three decisions we can
test. Do not copy competitors' wording, design, or proprietary frameworks.

**Done means:** a cited benchmark, a reusable issue scorecard, the six biggest
quality gaps, three strengths to protect, and three testable editorial changes.
At least one decision must improve usefulness, not merely presentation.

### BC-03: run an archive credibility-and-utility triage

Apply the new scorecard to every canonical issue. Flag unsupported personal or
numerical claims, stale evidence, broken tools or routes, intent overlap,
missing decision context, and weak next actions. Rank the top ten repairs by
reader harm and opportunity. Implement no more than two isolated repairs in the
first PR so changes remain reviewable.

**Done means:** every issue has keep, refresh, consolidate, or investigate
status; the first two repairs pass editorial validation and route checks; no
issue is rewritten merely because it is old.

### BC-04: build the flagship-format portfolio

Design three repeatable formats that make the publication recognisable:

1. **Decision Teardown** — a costly CS decision, evidence threshold, owner,
   trade-off, and failure mode;
2. **Operating System** — a practical tool, facilitation steps, and adoption
   check;
3. **Evidence Brief** — what credible sources agree on, what remains uncertain,
   and what an operator should test next.

Select one source-safe archive or approved upcoming issue for each format and
prepare a transformation brief. Preserve the canonical website as the full
home and keep LinkedIn adaptations useful without copying the full article.

**Done means:** three reusable briefs, one fully validated pilot package, a
clear audience promise, and a distribution route with separate attribution.
No fabricated case study, customer result, or first-person story.

### BC-05: create an original-evidence engine

Advance CG-19 from an idea to a reproducible research system. Start with one
costly problem that appears in at least two genuinely independent signal
classes. Define five neutral questions, sampling limitations, minimum role mix,
analysis code or method, consent language, partner recruitment plan, and the
practical artifact respondents receive.

**Done means:** two credible distribution partners are staged or committed and
the study can be reproduced from aggregate data. Do not launch or publish a
percentage headline below 30 qualified responses.

### BC-06: earn a collaboration portfolio, not isolated pitches

Complete CG-15 with eight verified operator newsletters or practitioner
publications that do not duplicate the active channel pipeline. Rank the top
three by audience fit, contribution format, canonical policy, and likelihood of
two-way distribution. Prepare one distinct, source-safe collaboration for each:
a guest evidence brief, a joint decision teardown, or an asset swap that is
useful without requiring an email gate.

**Done means:** three tailored proposals, exact entry routes, separate campaign
labels, one-follow-up dates, and stop rules. Search Gmail and the pipeline before
any send. No audience-size claims unless the publisher states them publicly.

### BC-07: establish a practitioner evidence council

Advance CG-17 by defining a small, transparent rotating council of experienced
CS operators who can challenge issue premises before publication. Participation
is voluntary, time-bounded, and credited only with permission. Start with a
single 20-minute async review prompt, not an advisory-board claim.

**Done means:** participation note, invitation copy, decision rubric, aggregate
response reporting, and three individually relevant candidates. No private
names in Git, testimonial harvesting, or implied endorsement.

### BC-08: make every distribution surface return value to the reader

Audit the next four approved issues and their LinkedIn Newsletter adaptations.
For each, verify one clear problem promise, one useful action in-platform, one
canonical next step, one working tool or reading path, and one distinct campaign
label. Repair only gaps that do not disturb the active CID-001 baseline or the
manager's approved calendar.

**Done means:** a four-issue continuity report, zero broken canonical/tool
routes, and a ranked post-baseline repair list tied to qualified actions and
subscriber retention rather than clicks alone.

### BC-09: create the weekly growth-and-quality review

Produce a compact aggregate review that joins editorial quality, distribution,
acquisition, activation, and retention without mixing causal claims. It must
separate LinkedIn posts, LinkedIn Newsletter, partner traffic, referral,
organic search, and direct traffic; flag minimum-evidence failures; and show the
one next decision for each active experiment.

**Done means:** one reproducible command or documented query path, no PII, no
pre-#90 inference, and explicit keep/change/stop decisions. Output volume and
follower count are context, never the primary success metric.
