## Start now: execution order

### CG-01: operate the weekday LinkedIn relationship loop

**Why now:** roughly 8,200 followers create attention, but the owned-site transfer is weak. Useful participation in active operator conversations can widen qualified reach without disturbing the Tuesday launch.

**Deliverable:** each weekday, find three current discussions from credible CS, SaaS, Product, RevOps, or founder operators. Check Kuber's existing comments on the exact post. Publish one substantive no-link comment on each thread. Log the public post URL, public comment URL, date, and operating topic in the shared `Engagement List` only.

**Quality bar:** add an operating distinction, implementation question, trade-off, failure mode, or counterexample. Agreement, praise, generic summaries, hashtags, emojis, and Churn Is Dead promotion do not count.

**Proof:** three verified live comments per weekday; aggregate weekly profile views, out-of-network reach, relevant inbound, and tagged site visits.

**Stop rule:** stop a thread after one useful comment unless another participant asks a real question. If five weekdays produce no relevant replies, profile movement, or site signal, change the target-account mix rather than increasing comment volume.

### CG-02: run a founder reply and comment-rescue pass

**Why now:** unanswered questions and substantive disagreement on Kuber's own posts are warmer than cold audience acquisition.

**Deliverable:** inspect recent Kuber posts and notifications. Answer up to five substantive unanswered questions or operating disagreements. Answer the person in the thread. Do not drop a link unless the question explicitly asks for the asset and the route is current, relevant, and tracked.

**Proof:** count of useful replies published, follow-on discussion, relevant inbound, and qualified site visits. Store counts and public URLs only.

**Stop rule:** no reply to praise-only comments, no resurrection of stale threads merely to create activity, and no more than one answer per person per discussion unless they continue it.

### CG-03: earn one new practitioner-audience route

**Why now:** Churn Is Dead needs borrowed distribution beyond Kuber's feed.

**Deliverable:** verify one non-duplicate CS publication, newsletter, podcast, community, webinar, or practitioner event using current first-party eligibility evidence. Prepare one distinct contribution around a Churn Is Dead operating system and send it only after searching the pipeline and Gmail for duplicates.

**Preferred angles:** the three-slide QBR Kuber personally built; the product-to-relationship reversal in the experience ledger; the Renewal Evidence Packet; decision rights between CS, Product, and Sales. Match one angle to the audience instead of reusing a generic pitch.

**Proof:** first-party route, tailored contribution, source-safe experience use, attribution label, sent or staged evidence, follow-up date, and one-follow-up stop rule.

**Stop rule:** one pitch and one useful follow-up. If the route is closed, pay-to-play, or requires unsupported claims, park it and move on.

### CG-04: build the newsletter-collaboration target map

**Why now:** a small number of trusted CS newsletters can transfer more qualified readers than generic directories.

**Deliverable:** identify eight active operator newsletters with audience fit, recent publication evidence, contributor or collaboration route, originality rules, canonical-link policy, and a concrete reason the audience would value one existing Churn Is Dead framework. Rank the top three and stage one highly tailored newsletter swap, guest note, or joint-edition proposal.

**Proof:** current first-party URLs for every target, no duplicate contact, one proposed value exchange, one campaign label, and one measurable owned-site destination.

**Stop rule:** do not pitch an unverified audience, buy a list, promise reciprocal praise, or duplicate an article in a way that weakens the canonical site.

### CG-05: prepare one community-native contribution

**Why now:** practitioner communities reward complete answers and punish link dumping. A useful native contribution builds authority before acquisition.

**Deliverable:** inspect 30 current practitioner discussions across at least four public communities or operator forums. Record aggregate recurrence by costly operating problem, without identities or quoted private text. Then find one rules-compliant discussion that maps to a verified Churn Is Dead operating system and write a complete answer that works without a link. If the community rules and question make a tool useful, place one tagged canonical link at the end; otherwise keep it link-free.

**Proof:** 30-thread aggregate signal map, current rules, discussion URL, contribution text, live or staged status, and aggregate replies or direct visits.

**Stop rule:** no gated lead magnet, no promotional bait, no repeated posting of the same answer across communities, and no invented community membership.

### CG-06: create the source-safe contributor kit

**Why now:** every accepted pitch currently requires the same due-diligence work. A small, factual kit reduces partner friction and improves acceptance speed.

**Proof:** every claim maps to the experience ledger or a public Churn Is Dead URL; editorial voice review passes; no unsupported numerical claim survives.

**Stop rule:** do not build a vanity speaker page until a partner asks for a public URL. Ship the reusable content first.
