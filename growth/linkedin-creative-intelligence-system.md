# LinkedIn creative intelligence system

**Objective:** turn Kuber's proven post mechanics, current public Customer
Success content signals and original format bets into qualified follower growth
without copying creators or chasing low-fit virality.

**Manager source:** [`15K Creative Lab`](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1bkwIU02ejHXb_fwazAIhGb1jdhlobhhdVpZAHqsYfWE/edit#gid=1646406001)

The shared Sheet is the execution brief. This file records the operating logic,
evidence limits and decision rules behind it.

The abstract ownership post produced only seven reactions and four comments. Do
not repeat that structure unchanged. Convert broad claims into a named failure,
an observable consequence and a clear decision owner.

The 24 August health-context post and 25 August Product Friction Review post
created substantive conversation but zero post-attributed followers in the
available reads. They remain useful authority and publication surfaces, but
should not be mistaken for follower engines until direct attribution changes.

## Creative portfolio

Across the next five comparable LinkedIn feed posts, use:

- two evolved versions of Kuber's own winning structures;
- one original adaptation of a current external pattern;
- one genuinely new format bet;
- one Churn Is Dead issue or public-tool post.

This mix does not add posts. It selects the strongest job for each existing
calendar slot. The native LinkedIn Newsletter remains separate and never causes
a duplicate Wednesday feed post.

Keep approved public copy unchanged through the 1 September CID-001 close. The
first portfolio starts after that clean baseline unless a safety or factual
correction is required.

## Visual system for the social manager

### Use Kuber's personal photos when

- the post is a leadership field note, a real change of mind, a genuine
  milestone or a time-bounded build lesson;
- the photo adds human context that the text cannot provide;
- the image is already approved for public use and contains no internal screen,
  customer data or confidential material.

Prefer natural speaking, event, desk or work-in-progress photos. Keep the photo
uncluttered and avoid large quote overlays. Cisco or Splunk context may appear
only when already public and relevant; do not add employer logos or imply a
formal designation that does not exist.

### Use a graphic or mock-up when

- the post contains a playbook, checklist, decision table, before/after example
  or annotated operating document;
- the artifact is genuinely usable on LinkedIn without clicking away;
- one visual idea can be understood on a phone.

Use a 4:5 vertical canvas, one strong headline, generous whitespace and the
Churn Is Dead navy, cream and red palette. For carousels, prefer five to seven
pages: hook, evidence or steps, failure/guardrail, practical takeaway. Show real
public playbook pages where possible, not a generic laptop mock-up.

Avoid robot imagery, glowing circuit brains, fake dashboards, stock teams,
decorative icon grids, tiny text and overdesigned quote cards.

### Use no asset when

- the opening is the creative idea;
- the argument is short, contrarian and easy to scan;
- a visual would merely restate the first line.

## Original format bets

The shared `15K Creative Lab` contains production briefs for six new formats:

1. **CS Decision Clinic:** a clearly labelled composite or public dilemma, then
   a before/after decision map.
2. **I changed my mind:** a genuine belief change, the evidence behind it and
   the new operating behaviour.
3. **Annotated operating memo:** a template or sanitised document with the
   decision risks marked up.
4. **Executive approval questions:** a saveable list of questions Kuber would
   ask before approving a CS or AI initiative.
5. **One-week leadership field note:** one externally safe APJC AI observation,
   implication and next move.
6. **Before/after teardown:** one material rewrite that makes an escalation,
   QBR, renewal note or AI handoff decision-ready.

## Weekly learning loop

1. On Monday, rank the prior seven days by post-attributed followers per 1,000
   reached, with profile viewers, saves, reposts and qualified comments as
   diagnostics.
2. Review Kuber's own top and bottom two structures and a small current public
   sample of high-fit CS posts. Record aggregate public evidence only.
3. Select the next five-post portfolio from the shared `15K Creative Lab`.
4. Give the manager one exact visual direction per post: personal photo,
   carousel/mock-up, or no asset. Do not request multiple decorative variants.
5. On Friday, make one `KEEP`, `IMPROVE`, `PIVOT` or `PAUSE` decision after the
   evidence gate in the 15K operating plan. Change one slot, not the entire
   calendar.

Below 5,000 combined members reached or ten post-attributed follows for a
comparison, report the pattern descriptively. Test each repeatable structure at
least twice before calling it a winner or loser. Virality is never guaranteed;
the operating outcome is qualified reach converted into relevant followers and
owned Churn Is Dead subscribers.
