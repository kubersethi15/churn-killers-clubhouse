## Every-workday lanes

These do not need to wait for the controlled acquisition experiment.

### Founder relationship loop

- The manager publishes five to seven substantive, no-link comments per weekday. The agent research lane may publish up to three additional high-value comments on separately claimed current posts from relevant CS or SaaS operators.
- Check Kuber's existing comments on the exact post first. One useful comment per thread is enough.
- Prefer operating distinctions, implementation questions, and counterexamples over agreement or praise. Apply the naturalness gate in `growth/channel-operating-system.md`; do not manufacture a typo pattern.
- Log only post URL, comment URL, date, broad topic, and aggregate weekly response. Never copy viewer or follower identities into the repo.
- Review profile views, relevant inbound messages, invitations, and out-of-network reach weekly.

### Partner and earned-audience loop

- Monitor open Gmail threads for replies and answer useful questions promptly.
- Follow up once on the date in `growth/channel-pipeline.csv`; never send duplicate nudges.
- Research one additional high-fit practitioner publication, podcast, community, directory, webinar, or co-hosted session at a time.
- Verify contribution rules from a current first-party source before preparing outreach.
- Pitch one defensible operating idea with a practical reader outcome. Do not claim audience size, customer results, or Kuber experience without a ledger entry.
- Record route, status, attribution label, follow-up date, originality requirement, and canonical-link treatment.

### Growth reliability loop

- Check the live signup path, reactivation path, welcome-email function, growth-event inserts, and admin aggregate dashboards after every related deployment.
- Monitor delivery, bounce, complaint, suppression, and webhook health. Do not resume weekly broadcast merely because an API call succeeds.
- Keep acquisition, current-active count, and exact day-30 retention separate.
- Investigate unexplained zeroes, sudden source shifts, or impossible rates before changing creative.

### Reply and demand-signal loop

- Classify inbound reader and partner replies by costly problem, not by person.
- Record aggregate counts only: problem, requested help, tool opened, conversation accepted, and commercial intent.
- Route repeated problems into the editorial portfolio and monetisation readiness ledger.
- Never publish private correspondence or customer details without explicit permission.

## Agent cadence

At the start of a work block:

1. pull `main`;
2. read the live dashboard, external replies, action log, pipeline, experiment register, and coordination board;
3. claim one high-leverage non-overlapping item;
4. write the metric and stop rule before changing anything.

At the end of a work block:

1. verify the actual current state;
2. update the coordination row and action log;
3. open a focused PR with tests and live evidence;
4. record the next review date;
5. leave another agent a concrete next action, not a vague recommendation.
