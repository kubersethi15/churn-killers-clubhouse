# External-channel launch kit

**Staged by Claude, 24 August 2026.** For a human (Kuber or the social team) to
post. These are channels an agent must not auto-post to: they detect and ban
promotion, and a misfire burns the account. The drafts are ready; a person
presses submit.

Each entry states its honest expected value so no one spends a one-shot channel
on a low-probability bet.

**Tracked link for all of these:**
`https://churnisdead.com/qbr-score?utm_source=SOURCE&utm_medium=community&utm_campaign=qbr_score_launch`
Replace `SOURCE` per channel (reddit, hackernews, ggr, etc.) so each is measured
separately. All clean of agent traffic after the #90 guard.

## 1. Reddit — r/CustomerSuccess

**EV: medium, one shot.** The subreddit is small but exactly on-audience. It
removes anything that reads as self-promo. The only version that survives is
genuinely useful on its own, with the tool as a footnote a reader has to want.
Post from an aged account with prior non-promo comments, never a fresh one.

**Title:** The QBR test I wish someone had given me earlier: does your review actually decide anything?

**Body:**
> Most QBRs I sat through early in my career were status updates in a nicer room. Everyone showed up, slides got presented, we agreed things looked good or concerning, and then nothing changed.
>
> The thing that fixed it for me was cutting the QBR down to one question: what decision does the customer have to make this quarter, and did the meeting make it. Everything that does not serve that decision is theater.
>
> The structure I landed on is three blocks. The customer's goal and whether the quarter aligned to it. What was achieved. What is next, including where you need support. Thirty minutes. The one-page plan is the meeting, not an attachment to it.
>
> I turned that into a short self-scoring checklist if it is useful, no signup for the result: [tracked link]. But the three blocks above are the whole idea, so you do not need it.
>
> Curious how other people here handle the QBR where the numbers are bad. That is the moment the whole thing either becomes honest or becomes a performance.

**Rule:** engage every reply for the first two hours. Do not drop the link again
in comments. If mods remove it, do not repost.

## 2. Hacker News — Show HN

**EV: low, high variance, one shot.** HN is not a CS audience, so most of the
traffic is unqualified. Worth a single attempt because a tool that says something
true about a business ritual occasionally catches, and the backlink is durable.
Post Tue-Thu, 8-10am US Eastern.

**Title:** Show HN: A two-minute test for whether your quarterly business review is theater

**Text:**
> I spent ten years in customer success watching quarterly business reviews that informed everyone and decided nothing. I built a small self-scoring tool that grades a QBR on how far it sits from forcing an actual decision. Eight questions, no signup for the result. The scoring is opinionated, based on a three-block format I used to replace the standard review. Happy to talk about the framework or the anti-pattern it targets. [tracked link]

**Rule:** answer every comment substantively, especially the skeptical ones. HN
rewards the author who defends the idea well.

## 3. Gain Grow Retain / CS community Slack

**EV: medium, relationship-gated.** Only works if Kuber is already a participating
member. Drop it in a relevant thread, not the general channel, and only where
someone is actually discussing QBRs or reviews. Never as a broadcast.

**Message:**
> On the QBR thread: the reframe that helped me was treating it as one decision, not a status update. If the customer does not leave having decided something, it was a report. I made a quick self-scoring version of the test if useful, no signup: [tracked link]. The core is three blocks though: their goal and whether the quarter aligned, what was achieved, what is next and where you need support.

## Honest ranking

If there is time for only one, do Reddit. It is the only channel here that is
both on-audience and open to a non-member. Hacker News is a lottery ticket with
a good backlink attached. The Slack only works if the relationship already
exists, which makes it the social team's call, not a cold drop.

None of these is the main engine. The main engine is the tool's own share loop
plus Kuber's owned audience. These are supplements, worth one clean attempt each
and not worth a campaign.
