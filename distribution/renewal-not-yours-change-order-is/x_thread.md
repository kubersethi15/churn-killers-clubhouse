# X Thread -- The Renewal Is Not Yours to Own. The Change Order Is.
# Post each tweet as a reply to the previous one

--- Tweet 1 ---
You don't own the renewal. You never did.

The renewal is a procurement event. Decided by whether your champion still has budget and still has a job.

What you actually own is the change order. And you gave it away for free.

--- Tweet 2 ---
One enterprise CSM I know absorbed 31 out-of-contract requests across 11 accounts in a single quarter.

The 9pm exec deck. The unpaid training. The integration triage her client's own engineer broke.

She called all of it "the relationship."

--- Tweet 3 ---
In professional services, every one of those is a priced change order. A documented variation. A line item someone signs.

CS imported the "do whatever it takes" ethos. And left behind the one discipline that made it survivable.

--- Tweet 4 ---
Here's the hard truth.

The unpriced favors that make an account love you are the exact same favors that make your role trivially outsourceable.

Generosity without a change order isn't a strategy. It's an unfunded liability with a smiling face.

--- Tweet 5 ---
The fix: start a favor ledger. Count every out-of-contract request for one quarter. Route it to product, to priced services, or to a documented no.

Keep the handful a machine can never fake.
